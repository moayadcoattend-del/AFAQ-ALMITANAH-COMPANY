About Afaq Al Mitanah (شركة أفاق المتانة)

Empowering the Kingdom's Growth with Scalable, Disciplined Human Capital

Afaq Al Mitanah is an established Saudi manpower supply contracting firm. We assist Tier-1 contractors, industrial operations, hospitals, and hospitality groups by deploying qualified, safety-trained, and fully documented workforce contingents on both short- and long-term contracts.

Our Strategic Mission

To eliminate labor shortages and project delays by delivering thoroughly verified tradesmen who uphold international occupational health, safety, and productivity standards.

Vision 2030 Alignment

Committed to supporting Saudi Arabia's national transformation pipeline with transparent digital contracts through Qiwa, high Saudization levels, and ethical worker welfare.

Turnkey mobilization including visa sponsorship, Iqama transfer, and camp logistics.
Practical trade testing in certified workshops before client site deployment.
Multilingual field supervisors ensuring direct communication with site engineers.
